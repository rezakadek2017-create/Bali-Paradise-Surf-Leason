Questions before you book

Do I need any experience to join?

Not at all! Most of our guests have never surfed before. We start on the sand with the basics, then head into gentle, beginner-friendly white water. Complete first-timers are our specialty.

Is it safe for kids and non-swimmers?

Yes. We teach in shallow, waist-deep water with a low student-to-instructor ratio and provide a buoyant soft-top board and rash guard. Every lesson is adapted to your age, comfort and confidence.

What should I bring?

Just swimwear, a towel, sunscreen and a smile. We provide the surfboard, rash guard and all the coaching. Leave valuables at your accommodation — the beach is casual and relaxed.

What if the waves or weather are bad?

The ocean changes daily, so we adjust lesson times to the safest tides. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you the best time to surf today — or reschedule for free if conditions aren't right.

How do I book a lesson?

Simply tap any WhatsApp button on this page. Tell us your date, group size and experience level — we usually reply within minutes and confirm today's best tide for you.
